I represent a single party\'s claim over a territory with argument structure. I hold the claimant (a TerritorialAgent), the claimed territory, the period of the claim, the argument type (treatyLaw, geography, geology, economy, culture, history, effectiveControl, utiPossidetis, elitism, or ideology), supporting evidence, a confidence level (asserted, uncertain, disputed, or false), whether the claim is overt or hidden, and the motives and arguments backing the claim. I am part of the Territorial Disputes and Claims Layer (Phase 1) based on the OTTAOA.1 framework.
