I formally model cultural heritage from the Cataldo and Rinaldi glossary aligned with UNESCO 1972. I hold a heritageType (architectural, intangible, natural, mixed), collections of monuments, sites, and groups of buildings, intangible heritage elements, an authenticity status (verified, disputed, unassessed), an identity string, and a conservationStatus (intact, atRisk, endangered, lost). I am part of the Territorial Planning and Cultural Landscape Ontology (Phase 9).
