I am a generic container for treaty texts, diplomatic notes, maps, reports, UN resolutions, and other information artifacts relevant to territorial disputes. I hold an artifactType (treaty, diplomaticNote, map, report, resolution, speech, measurements), a URL, the territory I am about, a truthValue (true, false, uncertain, or misleading), the issuing agent, and a validity period. I am part of the Territorial Disputes and Claims Layer (Phase 1) based on the OTTAOA.1 framework.
